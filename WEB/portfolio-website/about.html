Personal Biography

Gordon James Ramsay is a British chef and restaurateur, most known for his exceptional cooking skills in the culinary industry. Born in Johnstone, Scotland, him and his family moved to England where he developed a passion for cooking after a circumstance that prevented him from pursuing a future in football.

After earning a vocational diploma in hotel management, he began honing is culinary skills under the tutelage of several renowned chefs like chef Marco Pierre White and chef Albert Roux.

Over the course of his career, Ramsay goes on to earn multiple Michelin stars and become one of the most recognizable personalities in cooking programs. He is widely known for hosting television programs such as Kitchen Nightmares, Hell's Kitchen, and MasterChef.
