Gordon Ramsay

Michelin-Star Chef • Restaurateur • Television Personality

Personal Biography

Gordon James Ramsay is a British chef and restaurateur, most known for his exceptional cooking skills in the culinary industry. Born in Johnstone, Scotland, him and his family moved to England where he initially aspired to be a professional football player. However, a circumstance prevented him from pursuing a future in the sport. Ramsay went on to continue his education and earn a vocational diploma in hotel management. Through his perseverance and natural talent, he began honing his culinary skills under the tutelage of several renowned chefs.

Over the course of his career, Ramsay goes on to earn multiple Michelin stars and become one of the most recognizable personalities in cooking programs. He is widely known for hosting television programs such as Kitchen Nightmares, Hell's Kitchen, and MasterChef.
